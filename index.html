Sheet 01Existing conditions

Most studios run on WhatsApp, Excel and somebody’s memory.

RFI 01 · Who covers when they are on leave?

The work is beautiful. The system behind it is a group chat, a fee tracker in its seventh version, and one person who remembers everything.

  1. 1

    Fees leak between the drawing board and the invoice.

    A stage is finished. The client approves it on a call. Accounts hears about it weeks later, if at all. The work was done; the invoice was not raised.

  2. 2

    Nobody can see the studio’s real week.

    Who is on which project. How many hours a stage has already taken. Who is on leave on Thursday. The answers exist, in three sheets and two chats that do not agree.

  3. 3

    The system is a person.

    Every fee, every revision, every promise made to a client lives in one senior’s head. The day they are on site, the studio runs on guesswork.

None of this is a talent problem. It is a broken line.

So we drew it as one.

Design stages & deliverables

The contract’s milestones become the planner. Nobody types in a percentage: progress rolls up from finished tasks.

RefItemStatus

  1. P-01A planner in four levels: milestone → deliverable → task → sub-task. The milestones come from the contract.Live
  2. P-02Every task has owners, a status (To Do, In Progress, On Hold, Done), planned and actual dates, and remarks.Live
  3. P-03Progress rolls up by itself: a task counts only when Done; deliverables average their tasks; milestones weight their deliverables; the project weights its milestones by fee.Live
  4. P-04Deliverable weightage: how much each deliverable counts towards its milestone. The weights must total 100.Live
  5. P-05Drag to reorder; duplicate or move a deliverable with its tasks; select several tasks to duplicate, move or delete.Live
  6. P-06Project overview: milestones with contract, planned and actual end dates; areas by floor and size; the team by designation.Live
  7. P-07Budgeted hours by designation, against the hours used from approved timesheets.Live
  8. P-08One task list across every project (Past Due, This Week, Due Later, No Due Date), plus internal and personal tasks.Live
  9. P-09A daily email of each person’s open and overdue tasks, where your firm switches it on.Live
  10. S-07Project areas by floor and size, so the whole team names the building the same way.Live

Money

Nothing on a project’s finance page is typed in. It reads the contract, its invoices and receipts, and approved time.

RefItemStatus

  1. M-01Every client invoice is raised against a contract milestone, never from scratch.Live
  2. M-02Milestone status moves by itself: Not Due → Due → Payment Pending → Partial Received → Received.Live
  3. M-03A milestone becomes Due at 100 % completion, or when the client approval date is entered.Live
  4. M-04An invoice line cannot exceed the milestone’s fee less what has already been received.Live
  5. M-05A receipt fills its milestone allocation from the invoices it settles; an advance can be received first and reconciled later.Live
  6. M-06The project’s money on one page: fee, collected before GST, balance, timesheet cost and margin; In Budget, Alert or Cost Exceeded.Live
  7. M-07GST on every invoice: IGST or CGST + SGST from the place of supply, HSN/SAC on the lines, e-invoice (IRN) where required.Live
  8. M-08GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B data prepared for your CA; purchases reconciled against GSTR-2B for input tax credit.Live
  9. M-09The full books: purchases, journals, receivables and payables by age, ledger, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet.Live
  10. M-10Profit and loss for a single project, or for a single service line.Live

  • Opens the Dashboard: Landing, Projects, Finance, Human Resources and CRM, where the firm has switched it on.Live

Sheet 10Sequence of works

We set it up with your team.

Back to ink. Inspera does not arrive as a login and a manual. We configure it around the way your studio already works, bring your records in, and stay on.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We sit with your team and map how the studio runs: its stages, fee structures, approvals and leave rules.

  2. 02

    Set out

    We configure Inspera to match: contract templates by type of job, milestone lists, service categories, roles by designation, shifts, holiday lists and leave policies.

  3. 03

    Move in

    We bring your records in: people, running contracts with their milestones, and the invoices raised before Inspera.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Your team runs the studio on it. Ours stays on for support.

General notes

  1. 1Managed SaaS. Inspera runs in the browser; there is nothing to install or maintain.
  2. 2Hosted on AWS servers in Mumbai, India.
  3. 3Built on ERPNext, a mature open-source ERP. Finance, accounting, GST, payroll and HR run on a proven backbone we did not reinvent; Inspera adds the studio on top — contracts, milestones, the planner and the App.
  4. 4Your data stays yours. Lists and reports export to Excel or CSV.
  5. 5Your studio gets its own address, in the form yourstudio.inspera.live.
  6. 6In the Desk, every record keeps its history: who changed which field, from what to what, and when.Live
  7. 7Bulk import for the move: people, contracts with their milestones, tasks and attendance come in from a sheet, with a preview and an error log.Live
